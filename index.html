CASE STUDY / 024

Bay House Kitchen

LOCATIONCoral Gables
SCOPEFull kitchen renovation
DURATION8 weeks
STATUSComplete
PROJECT BRIEF

Open the kitchen visually without turning the entire first floor into one undifferentiated room. Add storage, improve lighting, and keep the material palette warm.

The constraint was useful.

Instead of removing every wall, the design kept a partial division and used millwork, sightlines, and light to make the kitchen feel larger while preserving distinct rooms.

DETAIL 01

Full-height oak storage

Storage moved to one uninterrupted wall so the working side of the kitchen could stay visually quieter.

DETAIL 02

Lighting as structure

Task, ambient, and decorative lighting were separated rather than relying on a single overhead layer.
